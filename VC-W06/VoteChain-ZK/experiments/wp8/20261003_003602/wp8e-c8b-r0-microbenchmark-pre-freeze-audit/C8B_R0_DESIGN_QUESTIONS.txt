WP8E C8B-R0 MICROBENCHMARK DESIGN QUESTIONS
============================================

This audit intentionally submits no transactions.

Before freezing the final microbenchmark runner, resolve from the frozen
protocol and existing V2 harness:

1. Exact iteration count:
   expected 5 warm-up + 20 measured.

2. Freshness boundary:
   confirm whether each iteration requires a completely fresh Anvil chain.

3. Vote fixture:
   identify the exact frozen ballot/proof fixture to use for every fresh-chain
   single-vote iteration.

4. Timed interval:
   confirm the metric remains cast-send-start to receipt-return only.

5. Excluded setup:
   confirm deployment, configure, activate, fixture loading, and post-receipt
   correctness reads are outside the timed interval.

6. Required per-iteration raw fields:
   warmup/measured identity, iteration number, transaction hash, receipt
   status, gasUsed, calldata bytes, and wall-clock latency.

7. Correctness gates:
   acceptedBallotCount == 1,
   usedNullifier == true,
   expected encrypted tally state,
   successful receipt.

8. Parser:
   reuse the frozen V2 leading-JSON receipt parser behavior, not the V1
   json.loads(full_stdout) assumption.

9. Statistics:
   final report must use only the 20 measured iterations and preserve all
   5 warm-ups separately.

10. Failure policy:
    preserve any partial attempted final campaign; do not silently restart or
    delete observations.
